### 🛰️ About me

I'm a network engineer with **17+ years** of building and modernizing the infrastructure that carriers and heavy industry run on — from nationwide **WiMAX / 3G / LTE** rollouts to **OT networks for mining automation**, **DOCSIS broadband** node migrations, and carrier **SIP / IPv6 voice core** work.

Through my consultancy, **Mercury IT**, I've delivered for clients including **Rogers, Shaw, and Rio Tinto**. I like the hard middle ground where networks, operations, and people meet: coordinating multi-vendor teams, owning budgets, and getting complex migrations over the line without breaking production.

These days I'm also deep into **self-hosted AI** — running and quantizing local LLMs on hardware I built myself.

---

### 🔧 What I work on

- 🏭 **OT / Industrial networking** — designed and supported the OT network for Rio Tinto's Diavik (DDMI) mine automation
- 📡 **Broadband & DOCSIS** — node migration and access-network work at Shaw
- ☎️ **Voice core & SIP** — soft-switch consolidation, PRI → SIP, and SIP-trunk migration from Ribbon to Metaswitch over an IPv6 telephony network at Rogers
- 🤖 **AI infrastructure** — helped build out Rogers' AI infrastructure and data-network engineering
- 📶 **Wireless / RAN** — LTE modernization for Rogers/Telus (Huawei), Freedom Mobile 3G swap & LTE (Nokia), 3G/transport for Orange, and the Atheeb WiMAX rollout
- 📋 **Project leadership** — multi-vendor coordination (Ericsson, Nokia, Huawei, NEC, Alcatel) and CAPEX/OPEX oversight
